How it works

One run. Five models. A concrete trade list.

Unsupervised learning, supervised learning, deep learning, classical portfolio theory and a large language model — chained into a single pass over your holdings.

01
K-Means Clustering
Unsupervised · scikit-learn
Profiles every asset as Growth, Moderate or Defensive from its return, volatility and Sharpe ratio — so you can see what each position actually contributes.
02
Random Forest Regressor
Supervised · per ticker
Trains a separate model per instrument on a rolling-window feature set to forecast next-quarter return, producing a Bullish / Neutral / Bearish signal.
03
MLP Neural Network
Deep learning · risk score
Reads the current market regime for your specific portfolio and emits a 0–1 risk score. That score directly tightens or loosens the position bounds the optimizer is allowed to use.
04
Markowitz / Modern Portfolio Theory
Optimization · SciPy SLSQP
Solves for the Max-Sharpe and Min-Variance allocations with Ledoit-Wolf covariance shrinkage, under bounds adapted to the risk score above.
05
Rebalancing Engine
Translation layer
Converts optimal weights into instructions you can act on: BUY $X, SELL $Y, HOLD — measured against the holdings you actually have today.
06
Claude AI Assistant
Haiku 4.5 · Anthropic
Explains the result in plain English, then answers follow-ups and takes commands — "add TSLA", "make it more defensive" — re-running the whole pipeline and reporting what changed.

Questions

Before you ask.

Why can't I just sign up?
Because the output is easy to misread. A 0.88 risk score or a "SELL $1,700" line means something specific about the models that produced it, and handing that to someone cold invites bad decisions. The walkthrough exists so you know what the numbers are and — just as importantly — what they aren't.
Is this investment advice?
No. PortfolioAI is a research and educational tool. Its predictions, risk scores and rebalancing suggestions come from statistical models trained on historical data and can be wrong. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and it is no substitute for a qualified financial professional.
What data does it run on?
A bundled adjusted-close price history covering 289 instruments from 2021-11-10 to 2026-07-02, built offline via yfinance. The Sharpe calculations assume a 5% risk-free rate. Because the history is bundled rather than streamed, results are reproducible — the same inputs give the same output.
Does it place trades or connect to my broker?
No. PortfolioAI never touches a brokerage account and has no execution capability. You enter your holdings by hand, and it returns a trade list for you to consider and act on yourself.
What happens to the data I enter?
Holdings you type into the terminal are sent to the optimization API for the duration of the run and are not stored as a portfolio record. Demo requests are stored so I can reply to you. Anonymous usage events are recorded to understand which features are used.
Can I see the methodology?
Yes — the full walk-through, from feature construction through the optimizer's constraints, lives in an open Jupyter notebook, and the codebase is MIT-licensed. Ask during the demo and I'll point you at the relevant sections.
Not investment advice
PortfolioAI is intended for educational and research purposes only. Predictions, risk scores and rebalancing recommendations are produced by statistical models trained on historical data and may be wrong. Do not use this software to make real investment decisions without consulting a qualified financial professional.
